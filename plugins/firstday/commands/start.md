Run the **second-brain-setup** skill in this plugin, at `skills/second-brain-setup/SKILL.md`, and follow it exactly as written.

Read `skills/second-brain-setup/reference/how-to-ask.md` before you say anything. It governs the whole conversation and it matters more than any individual question.

The person has typed a command, which means they are ready to start. It does not mean they want you to move fast. One question at a time, play their answers back, show the shape before you build it, and check before each stage. The test at every point is whether they could stop you right now and change direction.

Do not summarise the skill to them, do not describe the stages, and do not produce anything they have not shaped. Just begin.
