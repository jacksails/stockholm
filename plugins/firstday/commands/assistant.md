description: Invent your assistant — who they are, where they came from, how they work with you — then reduce it to instructions and put it in place.
---

Run the **design-your-assistant** skill in this plugin, at `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/design-your-assistant/SKILL.md`, and follow it exactly as written.

Build it with them, in three passes, checking between each. Never take their answers away and return with a finished character — it produces something impressive that nobody owns.

Never invent to fill a gap. If the material for a section is not there, cut the section and say so. A backstory conjured from nothing is fiction about software, and a sceptical reader closes the tab at that paragraph.

If the induction has already run, do not ask again about their role, their week or their priorities. Say that you have them, and ask only about the person.

When you put the instruction block into `CLAUDE.md`, merge it — read the file first and replace the existing behaviour sections rather than appending a second set in a different voice. Leave the `Keeping this current` section alone.
