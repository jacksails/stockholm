Run the **morning-brief** skill in this plugin, at `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/morning-brief/SKILL.md`, and follow it exactly as written.

Show them a sample brief built from their real day before you build anything. A brief they have seen and corrected is a brief they will still be reading in a month.

Be exact about what you can and cannot reach. If a connector is not available, say which one and what it would add — do not quietly produce a thinner brief and let them assume that is all there is.

Never claim the scheduled task is running unless it has genuinely been created. Tell them what will arrive, and when, and what to do if it does not.
